Detector: MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0 (COCO “person”), GPU delegate with CPU fallback. Tracker: constant-velocity Kalman filter with IoU association from factor-tracker. Solid boxes are confirmed tracks, dashed ones are not yet confirmed or briefly lost.
